VAT label by income

VAT becomes a label: A+ 5 %, A 11 %, B 17 %, C 25 %, special rate 33 % only for harmful goods. How strongly a household feels the change depends on how much it consumes and on what: rent, health, education and insurance are exempt.

Price level

One-off price level effect in 2028: 2.47 % mechanically, 1.2 to 2.1 % with partial pass-through and taxable consumption only. VAT increases are passed on to prices three to four times more strongly than cuts (Benzarti et al., JPE 2020); an asymmetric variant is open. Lower label rates are reached through certification, which may strengthen pass-through of cuts (not yet evidenced).
