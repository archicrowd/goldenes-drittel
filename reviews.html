Reviews

In October 2026 the website and the calculations were recalculated in three reviews. All findings are documented with their assessment and implementation; errors found are logged as numbered corrections.

The reviews were commissioned by the initiative. They do not replace independent assessment. The documents reflect the state of their version; later calculations may have changed figures in them. Current values: research kit. The reports of the first and second review are held by the initiative and will be added with the reviewers' consent. Documents are in German; English summaries above each list.

First review, 19 October 2026

Website and repository. The reviewer assessed the predecessor model (version B, 28 %) because our entry documents still named it as current – our error (correction 66). Main points: the pass-through of employer contributions is the weakest element; the level of the safety net was not quantified; a counter-proposal "rule book instead of a roof". Response: legal obligation to adjust employment contracts by the start date with a fallback rule; the net level is published; the variant was worked out.

  • Response of the initiative (v4.12) (PDF, German)
  • Reply to the reviewer (v4.13) (PDF, German)

Second review, 20 October 2026

The calculation core of the current model was re-implemented independently (household formulas, fund recursion, pass-through sensitivity) and confirmed. Findings B11–B16 concerned labelling and consistency: fund figures on the country pages, two dividend paths, communication of the pension cut, top-earner statements, total burden, size Z. A summarised report added an own microsimulation supporting "9 out of 10 full-time employees better off". Response: register of current figures (KENNZAHLEN), common fund on all pages, new calculations on price level, avoidance and the fallback rule.

  • Response to the second review (v4.14) (PDF, German)
  • Response to the summarised report (v4.17/v4.18) (PDF, German)
  • Reviewer's contribution: microsimulation "9 out of 10 employees" (PDF, German)

Third review, 24 October 2026

Model version 4.42 along the six questions of our review folder. The calculation core was confirmed at rounding level; eight findings (B17–B24), all inconsistencies or gaps, none a calculation error – among them three different values for the same total burden, the undocumented base of the company tax position, and missing growth sensitivity of the fund. Response: corrections 73–76, interest × growth grid, definition table of revenue positions; the base of the company position remains open.
