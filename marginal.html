Marginal burden, adjusted for what contributions buy

How much of an additional euro earned is kept? Comparing "new tax" with "today's tax" is misleading, because today health, long-term care and pensions are financed by contributions and in the new system by taxes. The calculation therefore adjusts for counter-values. So far computed for Germany.

Per cent per additional euro of gross wage, single, no children. Today: income tax, solidarity surcharge and employee contributions (2026 law); adjusted for the part of the pension contribution that creates an own entitlement (equivalence as a band, assumption). New: income tax, Germany's surcharge (15.0 %, after repayment 0) and the additional VAT burden. Compulsory saving (10 %) goes to an own, inheritable account and counts as saving; for comparison it is included in the last column.

Not included: the employer side, means-tested benefits and the top-up with earnings counted by half. In the lower range the marginal burden is therefore not yet fully computed (open task V1).
